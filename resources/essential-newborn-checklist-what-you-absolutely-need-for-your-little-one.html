Essential Newborn Checklist: What You Absolutely Need for Your Little One

Not medical advice. For emergencies, call your local emergency number.

Bringing home a newborn is an exciting time, but it can also be overwhelming to figure out everything you need. This guide breaks down the absolute essentials to help you prepare for your baby’s arrival. From feeding and sleeping to diapering and clothing, we cover the must-haves so you can focus on bonding with your new baby.

Feeding Essentials: * Bottles (if not exclusively breastfeeding) and nipples * Bottle brush and drying rack * Formula (if applicable) * Nursing bras and pads (if breastfeeding) * Nipple cream (if breastfeeding) * Burp cloths

Sleeping Essentials: * Crib or bassinet * Firm mattress and fitted sheets * Sleep sacks or wearable blankets (safe alternative to loose blankets) * Swaddle blankets

Diapering Essentials: * Diapers (newborn size) * Wipes * Diaper rash cream * Changing pad or changing table * Diaper pail

Clothing Essentials: * Onesies/bodysuits (short and long sleeve) * Pajamas/sleepers * Socks or booties * Hats * Sleep sacks (as mentioned above)

Bathing & Health Essentials: * Baby bathtub * Baby soap/wash * Soft washcloths * Baby towels * Baby lotion (optional) * Nail clippers or file * Thermometer * Infant pain reliever (consult pediatrician)

Gear & Travel Essentials: * Infant car seat * Stroller * Baby carrier or wrap * Diaper bag

Comfort & Soothing Essentials: * Pacifiers (optional) * Soothing music or sound machine

Important Note: It’s always a good idea to consult with your pediatrician for any specific recommendations or concerns. Remember, you don’t need everything on day one.
