Smart Sleep Coach Is Closing on November 30. Here Is Your Move Plan.

Not medical advice. For emergencies, call your local emergency number.

Smart Sleep Coach by Pampers stops working at 11:59pm EST on November 30, 2026. From December 1, the app and everything in it will no longer be accessible. Pampers has already stopped auto-renewals, and annual subscribers will be refunded for the unused months. The app has also been removed from download, so you cannot reinstall it later to check something. That gives you a short window to save what matters. Here is the order to do it in.

The short version

Screenshot your sleep plan first, because plans do not export. Then download your tracked data from Progress, then Patterns. Then set up your next app while Smart Sleep Coach still opens, so you copy from screenshots instead of memory at 3am. Snugs is one option: a browser based AI advisor and logbook with a 14-day free trial and no credit card required.

Step 1: Screenshot your sleep plan and schedule (do this first)

Your personalized schedule and sleep plan cannot be exported as a file. Pampers itself recommends taking screenshots of your baby's personalized schedule, sleep plans, or any other content you want to keep. Screenshot:

  • The current daily schedule, wake windows and bedtime
  • Your sleep plan and any settling steps in it
  • Any notes about what has been working

Step 2: Download your tracked data

The data you logged can be downloaded inside the app. Go to the Progress screen, open the Patterns view, and download your weekly and monthly data. Do it before November 30, because after that date the app and its content will not be accessible.

Step 3: Set up your next home before November 30, not after

Whichever option you choose next, set it up while Smart Sleep Coach still opens, so you can copy from your screenshots instead of working from memory at 3am. With Snugs, setup is a baby profile: age, feeding schedules, sleep habits, temperament, allergies and milestones. That profile is what Snugs uses to tailor its answers to your baby, and onboarding takes about 2 minutes. Snugs runs in your browser on iPhone, Android and desktop, so there is no new app to download.

What carries over, and what does not

Carries over, from your screenshots and downloads: your baby's current wake windows, nap pattern and bedtime; the sleep plan steps that have been working; your logged sleep history, as the weekly and monthly files you downloaded.

Does not carry over automatically: Snugs does not import a Smart Sleep Coach file. You recreate your profile from your screenshots, which takes a few minutes, and then log sleep, feeds, diapers and 10 other activity types in the Snugs logbook from there. Your downloaded Pampers files stay yours as your archive.
