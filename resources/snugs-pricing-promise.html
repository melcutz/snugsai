Our Pricing Promise: Your Baby's History Is Yours

Not medical advice. For emergencies, call your local emergency number.

In the last two months, parents using baby apps learned some hard lessons. A tracker that was free for years locked new entries behind a paywall, mid-crisis for some families. An app people had bought outright moved to a monthly subscription, with no grandfathering for the parents who already paid. And a sleep coach app announced it is shutting down completely, giving its families 28 days to screenshot their own sleep plans before everything goes dark.

“You're forcing people to subscribe to collect their data from their kids that you held access to until we gave you money.”

The Wonder Weeks App Store review, October 29, 2026, after a paid-once app moved to subscription

None of those parents did anything wrong. They just trusted that the history they were building, feed by feed and night by night, would stay theirs. We think that trust should be answered in writing. So here is ours.

The short version

Your baby's history is yours: exportable at any time, never moved behind a new paywall, never held hostage if you leave. If our pricing ever changes, existing customers keep their terms. If Snugs ever shuts down, you get notice and a full export window first.

The promise, in plain language

  • 1. Your history is always exportable. Export your logs and growth data as PDF reports, or export your data in full, at any time, whether or not you currently pay. No export fee, no retention call.
  • 2. No retroactive paywalls. Features you use today will not be moved behind a new paywall tomorrow. What you signed up for is what you keep.
  • 3. Grandfathering, always. If our prices ever change, existing customers keep the terms of the plan they joined on. Paying early is rewarded, not punished.
  • 4. No hostage data, ever. You can export or delete your account at any time. We would rather earn your renewal than block your exit.
  • 5. If we ever shut down, you hear it from us first. With notice, and a full export window before anything goes dark. Your baby's first year should never depend on our runway.

The mechanics that back it up

Promises are cheap, so here is the machinery. The 14-day free trial needs no credit card, which means a trial can never silently convert into a charge. You can cancel anytime and keep access until the end of the billing period. Your data is encrypted, stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure, and never sold or shared with third parties. And the export is not a support ticket: it is in the product, in your account, whenever you want it.

Why this page exists

We are a small, independent team, not a giant's side project. That cuts both ways, and we would rather be straight about it: small teams can disappear, which is exactly why your exit should be guaranteed while things are good, not negotiated when they are not. If you are moving to Snugs from an app that changed the deal on you, start with the guides below. If you are already here, hold us to this page.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is Snugs promising?

Four things. Your history is exportable at any time, free, whether or not you currently pay. Features you use are not moved behind a new paywall later. If our pricing ever changes, existing customers keep the terms of the plan they joined on. And if Snugs ever shuts down, you get notice and a full export window before anything goes dark.

Can I really export everything?

Yes. You can export your logs and growth data as PDF reports, and you can export your data or delete your account at any time from your account. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep access until the end of your billing period, and your data stays exportable. The 14-day trial needs no credit card, so a trial can never turn into a surprise charge.

Why publish a promise instead of just saying trust us?

Because in the last two months, parents watched a paid-once app move to a subscription with no grandfathering, a free tracker lock new entries behind a paywall, and a sleep app announce a full shutdown. Trust in this category is earned in writing, before anything goes wrong.
