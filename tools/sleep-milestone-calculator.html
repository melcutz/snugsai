    Frequently asked questions

    Is this schedule right for my child?
    These schedules reflect typical ranges many pediatric sources describe (wake windows and total sleep by age). Every baby is different, so treat them as a starting point and ask your pediatrician about your child.
    What if my child's routine doesn't match exactly?
    Shift times by 15–30 minutes to fit your family. Consistency matters more than the exact clock time.
    How do I drop a nap?
    Watch for signs (fighting naps, shorter naps, later bedtime) over 1–2 weeks, then push nap time later by 15–30 minutes every few days until it becomes quiet time.
