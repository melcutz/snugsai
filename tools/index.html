Frequently asked questions

Are the Snugs tools free?
Yes. Every tool on this page is free and needs no sign-up: enter your child's age or wake-up time and read the result.
Which tool should I use first?
Start with the wake window calculator if you want nap and bedtime times today, or the nap transition checker if you suspect a nap drop is coming.
What do the sleep milestone ranges mean?
They show typical AAP, CDC and National Sleep Foundation ranges by month, such as total sleep per day and overnight stretches. They are typical ranges, not targets.
Are these tools medical advice?
No. They are educational starting points based on published guidance. If you have questions about your child's sleep or development, ask your pediatrician.

Sources: American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) and CDC sleep guidance for toddlers, plus National Sleep Foundation (NSF) ranges for children aged 1–3. Not medical advice — these are typical ranges, not targets. If you have questions about your child’s sleep or development, ask your pediatrician.
