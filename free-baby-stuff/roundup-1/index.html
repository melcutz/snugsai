Free Baby Stuff Weekly #1: a free book every month, sample boxes, and the registry box truth

Welcome to the first roundup. This week we checked eleven popular "free baby stuff" offers on the brands' own websites. Four are genuinely free. Four are free only after you spend through a registry. Three did not make the list at all. Here are the highlights.

Start here: a free book every month until age 5. Dolly Parton's Imagination Library mails your child a free book every month from birth to age five, no card and no purchase. The only catch is geography: a local partner has to run it in your ZIP code. Checking takes thirty seconds and it is the best offer on this page by a mile.

Two sample boxes worth joining. PINCHme lets you build a box of four samples you qualify for, shipped free, and baby products appear regularly. SampleSource runs seasonal drops a few times a year with free boxes matched to your profile. Both ask for feedback or patience instead of money.

The registry box truth. Amazon, Target, Walmart and Babylist all advertise free welcome boxes. All four require purchases through the registry first: $10 at Amazon (with Prime) and Target, $25 through your Walmart registry link, and $30 plus $8.95 shipping at Babylist. If the spending was happening anyway, claim the box. Just do not let a "free" box talk you into a registry you did not want.

Not listed, on purpose. The Huggies free diaper box is Canada only right now. Pampers has no free sample signup at all. And Similac quietly replaced its sample program with a points program, whatever older blog posts still claim.
