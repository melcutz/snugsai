Registry welcome boxes are marketed as free. They are not, quite: each one asks you to spend a set amount through that store's registry first. If you were going to buy those things anyway, the box is a genuine bonus. Here is exactly what each one costs you.

A box of full-size, travel-size and sample products for parent and baby.

What it takes: an active Amazon Prime membership, a baby registry, 10 items added from the registry checklist, and over $10 of purchases made from the registry (you or your gift-givers). US shipping only, one box per household at a time.

Where: United States.

Verified November 11, 2026. Amazon's welcome box terms

Free with a catch

Target Baby Box (Registry Welcome Kit)

Samples and coupons Target values at $100: diapers, wipes, bottles, pacifiers and more.

What it takes: Target Circle (free to join), a baby registry, 10 items added, and over $10 of purchases from the registry. A coupon then appears on your registry dashboard within a day or two. One per year.

One flag from us: some parents report a $6.99 shipping charge at claim time. We could not find that fee in Target's own product description, so budget for the possibility and tell us if you see it.

Where: United States.

Need-based

La Leche League: free breastfeeding help

Free mother-to-mother breastfeeding support from accredited volunteer Leaders: local group meetings (in person and online), one-to-one help by phone, email or online, and free information. Some regions also run free antenatal breastfeeding classes. Memberships and donations exist but are never required for help. Coverage depends on volunteers near you.

Verified November 11, 2026. La Leche League International

Need-based

WIC: food support for mothers and young children

WIC provides a monthly package of nutritious foods (including infant formula and baby food), nutrition education, breastfeeding support and healthcare referrals. It is a US government program, and it does not cover diapers.

You qualify only if all of these hold: you are pregnant, recently postpartum, or breastfeeding, or your child is under 5; your household income is at or below 185% of the federal poverty line (for example $61,050 a year for a family of four, on the table in force July 2026 to June 2027), or you already receive SNAP, Medicaid or TANF; you live in the state where you apply; and a health professional at the WIC clinic assesses a nutritional risk.

Where: United States (all states, DC and territories), via local WIC agencies.

Verified November 11, 2026. WIC, USDA

Need-based

Free diapers: the National Diaper Bank Network

Over 240 community diaper banks across the US give free diapers to families who are struggling, usually through partner food pantries, clinics and shelters. There is no national eligibility rule: each local bank sets its own area, schedule and amounts, and amounts are a supplement, not a full month's supply. Stock is not guaranteed. You can also dial 211 to find help locally.

Where: United States.

Verified November 11, 2026. Find a diaper bank

A box of essentials for your baby's first six months: clothes from newborn to 6 months, a play mat, books, a digital thermometer, a hooded towel, muslins, and a mattress with fitted sheets, because the box itself is designed as a safe sleep space.

How it works: your midwife completes the registration card with you at your 18 to 20 week or 28 week appointment, and the box arrives about four weeks before your due date. No income test.

Where: Scotland, for babies born and living in Scotland to parents living permanently in Scotland.

Finland's Maternity Package

The original baby box, running since 1937. A package of baby clothes and care products, in a box with a mattress that works as a first bed, or the cash equivalent instead: 210 euros, tax-free. Expecting twins or more means multiple grants, and you can mix package and cash.

The gates are timing and residency, not income: you must live permanently in Finland (or work there and be covered), have a medical examination before the end of the fourth month of pregnancy, and apply at least two months before your due date. Packages cannot be sent outside Finland.

Where: Finland.

Section 8: Free and funded childcare

The biggest money on this page. The rules are exact, so we print them exactly.

Free with conditions

England: funded childcare hours

Two different offers, often confused:

Every 3 and 4 year old in England gets 15 hours a week of funded childcare for 38 weeks a year, whatever the parents earn.

Working parents can get 30 hours a week for 38 weeks, from the term after their child turns 9 months until school age. The gates: both parents (or a single parent) must be working or on qualifying leave, each expecting to earn at least about 16 hours a week at minimum or living wage over the next three months, and neither parent can have an adjusted net income over £100,000 a year. You apply from when your baby is 23 weeks old and reconfirm every three months. Providers may charge for meals, nappies and extras, but you can decline the extras and keep the hours.

Where: England (Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland run different schemes).

Verified November 11, 2026. GOV.UK: free childcare if working

Free with conditions

Scotland: 1,140 hours of funded early learning

Every 3 and 4 year old in Scotland gets up to 1,140 hours a year of funded early learning and childcare: roughly 30 hours a week in term time, or about 22 hours spread across the year, at council nurseries, partner nurseries and childminders, with a free meal on attendance days. No work test and no income test.

Some 2 year olds qualify too, mainly where a parent receives certain benefits (for example Universal Credit with monthly household take-home pay of £885 or less) or the child is care-experienced.

Where: Scotland.

Verified November 11, 2026. mygov.scot: funded early learning and childcare

Free with conditions

Head Start and Early Head Start (United States)

Free early learning and development for young children in low-income families: Early Head Start from birth to age 3 (many programs also support pregnant women), Head Start Preschool from 3 to 5, in centres, family childcare or your home, at no cost.

Eligibility: household income at or below the federal poverty line, or automatic if your child is in foster care, your family is experiencing homelessness, or you receive TANF, SSI or SNAP. The hard truth to know before you apply: programs are officially oversubscribed, so waiting lists are normal. Apply through your local program.

Where: United States, all states and territories.

Verified November 11, 2026. Head Start: how to apply

Free with conditions

Not free, but worth knowing: Canada's $10-a-day childcare

Canada's federal childcare program is real and it does cut fees a lot, but it is a discount, not a freebie, so it does not get a free listing on this page. Fees now average $10 a day or less in 8 of 13 provinces and territories, for regulated childcare spaces for under-6s; other provinces average at least 50% off. Parents still pay roughly $200 or more a month for full-time care. If you are in Canada, it is still the first childcare scheme to look at.

Where: Canada.

Verified November 11, 2026. Canada.ca: child care fee reductions

Section 9: Free days out

Free with conditions

Museums for All: museums from free to $5 with an EBT card

Show a SNAP/EBT card at more than 1,600 museums, children's museums, science centres, zoos, aquariums and gardens across the US and get in for free to $5 per person, up to four people per card, any normal operating day.

The catch: it is only for SNAP/EBT holders, and each museum sets its own price inside the free to $5 range, so check your museum before you go.

Where: United States, all 50 states.

Verified November 11, 2026. Museums for All

Free with conditions

Every Kid Outdoors: a free national parks pass for 4th graders

Every US 4th grader (or home-school equivalent) can earn a pass covering entry to more than 2,000 federal lands and waters for their school year. At per-person sites it covers all children under 16 plus up to three adults; at per-vehicle sites it covers everyone in your car. Complete a short online activity, print the paper voucher, and bring the paper: photos of it are not accepted.

The dates matter: the 2026 to 27 pass expires August 31, 2027. It does not cover parking, camping or state parks.

Where: United States, federal lands only.

Verified November 11, 2026. Every Kid Outdoors

Free with conditions

Your library card may include free museum and zoo passes

Many public libraries run a culture pass scheme: reserve a pass with your library card and get free entry to local museums, zoos and gardens. New York's Culture Pass covers 100+ venues for NYPL, Brooklyn and Queens cardholders; Contra Costa County's Discover & Go does the same in the San Francisco East Bay.

The catches are local: it is city by city, not a national program, you usually need a full resident library card (eCards often do not count), passes are scarce and booked out fast, and some systems include low-cost as well as fully free passes. Search your own library's website for "culture pass" or "museum pass".

Where: Many cities in the United States and elsewhere; terms set by each library system.

Verified November 11, 2026 (two systems checked in detail). NYC Culture Pass · Discover & Go

Free with conditions

Kids Bowl Free: two free games a day, all summer

Register your child at a participating bowling centre and they get two free games every day through the summer program. Centres take part across the US and Canada.

The catches are per centre: each centre sets its own age limit, season dates, days and times, so check yours before promising anything. And shoes are not included: bowling shoes are mandatory and rental is at the normal rate (one centre we checked charges $4.75), so a visit is cheap, not zero.

Where: United States and Canada, at participating centres.

Verified November 11, 2026. Kids Bowl Free

Truly free

CDC Milestone Tracker app

The CDC's free app for tracking development from 2 months to 5 years: illustrated milestone checklists with photos and videos, tips, a summary you can save or email, and well-visit reminders, in English and Spanish. The CDC states it does not collect or share identifiable information. It is a tracker, not a medical screening; raise concerns with your child's doctor.

Where: United States (CDC); app in English and Spanish.

Verified November 11, 2026. CDC Milestone Tracker

Truly free

Vaccines for Children (VFC): free vaccines for eligible kids

Every recommended childhood vaccine, at no charge for the vaccine itself, for children 18 and under who are uninsured, Medicaid-eligible, American Indian or Alaska Native, or underinsured (underinsured children must use a federally qualified health centre, rural health clinic or deputized site). Around 37,000 providers take part.

One nuance, printed because it matters: a provider may charge a state-capped administration fee, and office-visit fees can apply for non-Medicaid families, but a provider cannot refuse to vaccinate your child because you cannot pay the administration fee.

Where: United States and territories.

Verified November 11, 2026. CDC: Vaccines for Children

Truly free

Reach Out and Read: a free book at every checkup

At participating pediatric clinics, your child gets a brand new book to take home at every well-child checkup from birth to age 5, more than a dozen books in total, in 38 languages. The program runs at 6,753 sites in all 50 states.

The catch: the book comes with the checkup. There is no mail or walk-in route, and the checkup itself is ordinary pediatric care, billed as usual. The book is the free part.

Where: United States.

Verified November 11, 2026. Reach Out and Read

Truly free

Free smoke alarms, installed

United States: the Red Cross Sound the Alarm program sends volunteers to install free smoke alarms in your home, with escape planning and safety advice. Special alarms for Deaf and hard of hearing households are also free. Enter your ZIP on the Red Cross site to reach your local chapter; availability depends on where you live, and there is no national income test.

England: every fire and rescue service offers free home fire safety visits, and many will provide and fit free alarms during the visit. There is no single England rule: some services book for any resident (Essex), others prioritise people at higher risk (London, West Sussex), so check your local fire and rescue service.

Where: United States (Red Cross, by ZIP); England (by local fire service).

Verified November 11, 2026. Red Cross Sound the Alarm · Essex Fire: book a home safety visit

Truly free

Postpartum Support International: free help after birth

A free helpline (call or text 1-800-944-4773; Spanish text line 971-203-7773), more than 50 free online support groups in English and Spanish, a free app, and weekly expert calls. No diagnosis needed. It is not a crisis line: you leave a message and a trained volunteer calls or texts back the same day.

One warning that has to ship with this entry: when you register for a group, the booking platform shows a payment page for its own unlimited plan. PSI's own instruction is to skip it. The groups are free.

Where: United States based; coordinators in Canada, Mexico and 40+ other countries; groups are online only.

Verified November 11, 2026. Postpartum Support International

Section 3: Not listed here, and why

We leave offers off this page when they fail the honesty test:
